# ABC-Company-Employee-Analysis
data analysys of employee company
This project analyzes employee data from ABC Company using Python and visualization libraries. It involves data cleaning, exploration, and generating business insights to assist in workforce planning and salary allocation.

🔧 Preprocessing Steps
Replaced inconsistent values in the Height column with random integers between 150–180 cm to ensure uniformity.

Checked and handled null values in the dataset.

Dropped irrelevant columns to maintain data integrity and focus the analysis.

📈 Analysis Tasks
1. Team Distribution
Insight: The team with the largest number of employees is New Orleans Pelicans.

Visualization: Bar plot showing employee count per team.

2. Position Segregation
Insight: The most common position among employees is SG (Shooting Guard).

Visualization: Count plot of employee positions.

3. Predominant Age Group
Insight: Most employees are in the 20–30 age group, indicating a relatively young workforce.

Visualization: Bar plot of binned age groups.

4. Highest Salary Expenditure
Insight: The Los Angeles Lakers, specifically the SF (Small Forward) position, have the highest total salary expenditure.

Visualization: Bar chart grouped by team and position for total salary.

5. Age vs. Salary Correlation
Insight: A moderate positive correlation (r = 0.211) exists between age and salary, suggesting that older employees may earn more due to experience or seniority.

Visualization: Scatter plot and regression line.

📊 Graphical Representations
Visualizations were created using:

Matplotlib

Seaborn

Charts include bar plots, count plots, and scatter plots with regression overlays to clearly illustrate insights.

🔍 Key Insights
Team Focus: New Orleans Pelicans employs the most staff.

Role Trend: SG is the most held position.

Age Demographics: 20–30 is the predominant age group.

Salary Load: Highest expenditure comes from SF players in Los Angeles Lakers.

Age-Salary Relation: Older employees tend to have higher salaries, with a modest correlation of 0.21.
